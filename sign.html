INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the "Agreement"), dated as of the Effective Date (as defined below), is entered into by and among AGENCY NAME, a Florida limited liability company operating as a General Agency ("GA"), and , an Individual (the "Agent"). Required

RECITALS

A. WHEREAS, GA is a limited liability company organized under the laws of Florida that is engaged in the business of providing life insurance solutions;

B. WHEREAS, Agent is a duly licensed insurance agent whose National Producer Number (NPN) is ; and Required

C. WHEREAS, Agent desires to contract with GA to serve as an independent insurance agent of GA, and GA desires to contract with Agent, whereby Agent will serve as an independent agent of GA, pursuant to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

D. NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereby agree as follows:

AGREEMENT

1. Engagement. GA hereby engages Agent and Agent hereby accepts the engagement of GA to serve as an independent insurance agent of GA in connection with the insurance products sold by Agent (the "Engagement") pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.

2. Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall thereafter continue for a period of one (1) year, unless terminated earlier pursuant to the remaining provisions of this Agreement (the "Term"). This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms following the conclusion of the original Term unless either party provides written notice of its intent to terminate this Agreement within fifteen (15) days of the conclusion of the current Term.

3. Independent Contractor. Agent shall be retained as an independent contractor and shall not be considered under the provisions of this Agreement or otherwise as having employee status. Agent acknowledges and agrees that they are not an employee of GA for any purpose, including but not limited to payroll and employment tax withholding and reporting. Agent agrees to pay any and all federal, state, and local taxes of Agent, including self-employment taxes levied on account of the services performed by Agent. Agent also understands that they are not entitled to employment compensation, unemployment compensation and/or workers compensation and hereby waive any claim to such.

4. Expenses. All expenses incurred by Agent in performing under this Agreement shall be exclusively borne by Agent, except as specifically provided in this Agreement. Agent shall have no claim or shall not be entitled to reimbursement for any expenses, unless incurring such expenses was expressly authorized in writing by GA.

5. Control of Business. During the term of this Agreement, Agent shall retain sole and exclusive control over the manner, method, and means by which Agent performs services for GA, consistent with Agent's independent contractor status. While GA may define the scope of general services to be performed, GA shall not interfere with or direct the specific manner, time, or place of Agent's activities, except as expressly permitted herein. Recognizing that the insurance industry operates within a complex regulatory framework, GA reserves the right to establish and enforce reasonable standards of conduct, operational procedures, and compliance protocols necessary to ensure adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and industry best practices. Agent acknowledges that compliance with these standards is a material condition of this Agreement.

6. Appointment. Once Agent and GA fully execute this Agreement, Agent shall be eligible to be appointed in their individual personal capacity to procure applications for insurance contracts on behalf of the individual insurance companies GA is contracted with. Notifications of appointment will be given via email. Agent agrees not to solicit applications for insurance contracts on behalf of an insurance company until Agent receives notification of appointment and is licensed to sell the applicable insurance products in the State of sale, and has all other required certifications/authorizations.

7. Representations and Warranties. Agent represents and warrants that Agent has never been (i) an officer, director, or partner in a firm involved in any bankruptcy or receivership proceedings; (ii) a party to any civil action filed within the three (3) years prior to the Effective Date; (iii) arrested or convicted for any crime or misdemeanor other than a traffic misdemeanor; (iv) the subject of an order entered by any state or federal agency finding a violation of laws, rules or regulations; or (v) involved in any other action which could adversely affect the reputation of GA. Agent agrees to abide by all policies, practices and procedures adopted by the Company that are mandated by law, regulation, administrative bulletin or FAQ, or binding industry requirement.

8. Payment of Commissions. All commissions for insurance products sold under this Agreement will be paid directly by the insurance carriers to the Agent, in accordance with the commission levels established by GA. GA retains the sole and absolute discretion to determine, amend, or modify commission levels at any time without prior notice. The Agent is solely responsible for staying informed of the current commission schedules. GA shall bear no liability for any discrepancies, delays, or disputes concerning commission payments, which remain the sole responsibility of the carriers.

9. Chargebacks and Agent Liability. The Agent assumes full and exclusive responsibility for all chargebacks arising from cancellations, lapses, rescissions, or any other events resulting in the reversal of previously paid commissions, whether during or after the term. The Agent agrees to indemnify and hold harmless GA against any and all liabilities, losses, damages, costs, or expenses (including attorneys' fees and court costs) incurred as a result of the Agent's actions, inactions, omissions, or breach. GA reserves the right to deduct chargebacks from future commissions, and the Agent agrees to remit any shortfall within three (3) business days of written notice.

10. Personal Guarantee. As a material condition of this Agreement, the Agent shall execute and be bound by a Personal Guarantee, personally obligating the Agent to reimburse GA for any and all chargeback amounts, costs, fees, or damages that roll up to GA as a result of the Agent's actions, inactions, omissions, or failure to perform. The Personal Guarantee is irrevocable and survives termination or expiration of this Agreement until all outstanding liabilities are fully satisfied.

11. Termination. This Agreement may be terminated by either party, with or without cause, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. GA reserves the right to terminate immediately and without prior notice in the event of the Agent's breach of any material provision, misconduct, failure to perform, or any act or omission that adversely affects GA's reputation, operations, or compliance. Termination shall not relieve the Agent of obligations related to chargebacks, reimbursements, or indemnities.

12. Events After Termination. Upon termination, the Agent shall: (a) cease all representations of affiliation with GA; (b) return or destroy all confidential and proprietary information within five (5) business days; (c) settle all outstanding financial obligations; (d) comply with all restrictive covenants; and (e) acknowledge GA may withhold final commission payments for up to one (1) year to account for pending chargebacks or disputes.

13. Indemnification. Agent agrees to indemnify, defend and hold GA, its subsidiaries, and their respective officers, directors, shareholders, and employees harmless from all claims, expenses and liability (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising out of or connected with Agent's performance, gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of obligations under this Agreement.

TrueCare Sales Agent Handbook

Independent Contractor (1099) Handbook — Sales Agents Only

Effective Date: —   Version: 1.0

This handbook outlines the standards, expectations, and policies that apply to all 1099 Sales Agents working with TrueCare ("the Company"). It exists to protect clients, protect the Company, and ensure a consistent, compliant, high-performing sales operation. This handbook may be updated at any time; agents may be required to re-acknowledge the most current version.

Key policies (summary; full handbook on file):

1) Equal Opportunity / Anti-Harassment. TrueCare maintains a professional environment free from discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. Concerns are reported to the Sales Director or HR.

2)–3) Accommodations; Scheduling. Reasonable disability/religious accommodations are considered. TrueCare provides company scheduling (agents do not self-schedule), with defined OEP/SEP/AEP office hours, minimum availability, and tardiness / no-call-no-show standards. Camera on for meetings; no calls from public places.

4) Compensation, Performance, Coaching. Commissions are paid weekly or bi-weekly based on carrier paid status. Carrier reversals may affect payouts. Disputes must be submitted within 24 hours of payout notification. QA scores below 70% trigger coaching and remediation.

5)–7) Client Data, Security, Tools & QA. Agents must protect all client PII/PHI and may not store client data outside approved systems. Hard rule: no client data on personal devices, notes apps, personal Google Drive, spreadsheets, or external CRMs. Approved systems: GoHighLevel (CRM) and JustCall/TLD (dialer). All calls run through the approved dialer with recording disclosure; minimum 5 QA calls reviewed per agent per week.

8)–10) Conflicts; Time Off; Separation. Agents may only sell within TrueCare. Lead brokering, side deals, and moving clients are prohibited. Time off requires two (2) weeks' notice; AEP and OEP are blackout periods. Upon offboarding, access removal is immediate and confidentiality obligations continue.
